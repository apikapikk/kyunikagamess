# Voxel Animals: Farm, Forest & Wild - 71 Rig-Ready Animals

**71 chunky, cute voxel animals from the barnyard, the woods, the savanna, the poles, the sea and the jungle,
plus birds, bugs and baby farm animals.** Every animal comes split into rig-ready body parts (legs, head, tail,
wings, fins, claws, arms) with pivots, so you can make them walk, swim, peck and wag in minutes.

![hero](preview/hero.jpg)

## What's inside

**Farm (13):** cow, pig, piglet, sheep, lamb, chicken, duck (mallard), turkey, horse, donkey, goat, dog, cat
**Farm babies (7):** calf, foal, goat kid, chick, duckling, puppy, kitten
**Forest (13):** deer (stag), fawn, moose, bear, wolf, fox, wild boar, rabbit, squirrel, owl, beaver, hedgehog, badger
**Savanna (8):** lion, elephant, giraffe, zebra, rhino, hippo, cheetah, meerkat
**Arctic (6):** polar bear, penguin, seal, walrus, arctic fox, reindeer
**Ocean & coast (7):** dolphin, orca, shark, sea turtle, crab, octopus, seagull
**Jungle (7):** tiger, monkey, gorilla, panda, frog, crocodile, snake
**Birds (6):** parrot (macaw), toucan, eagle, flamingo, peacock, snowy owl
**Bugs (4):** ladybug, bee, butterfly, snail

- **Free core (CC0, 15):** cow, pig, sheep, chicken, deer, fox, rabbit, owl, calf, lion, penguin, dolphin,
  frog, parrot, ladybug
- **Full pack (paid):** all 71 animals
- Cute but natural proportions, readable at 128 px, one shared 63-colour palette
- 1 voxel = 0.1 m (bugs: 0.02 m via `voxel_size_m`), origin at the feet, models face +Z, 9 to 50 voxels tall
- **Rig-ready parts:** each animal also ships as `<animal>__<part>` meshes (`body`, `head`,
  `leg_fl/fr/bl/br` or `leg_l/r`, `tail`, `wing_l/r`, `fin_l/r`, `claw_l/r`, `arm_l/r`, `arm_1..8` for
  the octopus, a `seg_1..3` + `neck` chain for the snake, `shell` for the snail) with joint pivots, offsets
  and parents in `manifest.json`. Parts reassemble the full animal exactly.
- Low poly: about 300 to 1,900 triangles per animal (greedy meshed)
